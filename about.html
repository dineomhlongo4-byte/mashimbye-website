OUR STORY

Making every journey

Mashimbye Tours is a transportation and tours service based in Mkhuhlu, Mpumalanga. We are committed to helping individuals, families and groups get where they need to go.

From tours and transfers to wedding and event transport, we aim to make arranging your journey straightforward and your time on the road comfortable.

Our promise is simple: treat every journey with care, communicate clearly and put your travel needs first.
